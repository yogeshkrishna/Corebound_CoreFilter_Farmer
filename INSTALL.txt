CEILING SCOUT 0.4.6 — PHONE UPDATE

1. In your existing Ceiling Scout app, tap Check for updates > Download & install.
   Version 0.2.0 after the repository rename: choose Update source, enter
   yogeshkrishna/Corebound_CoreFilter_Farmer, then Save & check. Only needed once.
2. Confirm Install in Android's installer. If needed, allow installs from Ceiling Scout once.
   Install over the old app. Do NOT uninstall: your build and calibration stay saved.
   First install: https://github.com/yogeshkrishna/Corebound_CoreFilter_Farmer/releases/latest > Ceiling-Scout.apk.
3. Open Ceiling Scout > Show floating bar > Open Corebound.
4. Choose a mode in the app: Use farmer, or Map while I play.
   Select Lost Scrapyard boosted to Frozen five stars.
   Farmer: press Run. Manual mapping: press Record, play and inspect the ceilings yourself, then Save.
   Default: three Hookshots / seven jumps. Change equipment through Edit build & farming settings.
5. Build, Calibrate and Stop are under the bar's ••• menu. Calibrate once if needed.
   Preview, compatibility capture and Saved maps & Wi-Fi transfer are in the app.
   Manual mapping saves completed runs automatically, and Save keeps unfinished routes.

FUTURE UPDATES

IF CONTROLS ARE DISCONNECTED
Enable Ceiling Scout controls in Android Accessibility. Android may first require
App info > menu > Allow restricted settings for a sideloaded app.

Pause prevents new touches; an already issued gesture releases within 700 ms.
Stop & hide ends the session. Reopen the bar from the app.
Supervise the first updated runs. The changes pass recorded-frame and logic checks;
they still need a phone trial. Unknown ads or ambiguous filter offers may pause.

SAVED MAPS ON YOUR LAPTOP
After a cleared, failed or paused run, open Saved maps & Wi-Fi transfer > Start Wi-Fi transfer.
Connect the laptop to the same Wi-Fi and open the link shown on the phone.
Keep the phone's map page open. Download the receiver from the laptop page and run it
as described there. It saves ZIPs in Documents\Ceiling Scout Maps, verifies them,
then removes only the corresponding verified phone copies.
Each ZIP includes map.png (picture) and map.json (raw observations/build/confidence).
Unknown areas remain blank. Fixed-pool identification is for a future iteration.

Version 0.4.6 fixes capture orientation and squeezing. Compatibility capture is
enabled automatically once. Open Corebound in landscape and use Preview to check it.
See README.md, docs/map-export.md and docs/verification-0.4.6.md for details.
