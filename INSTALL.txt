CEILING SCOUT 0.2.0 — PHONE UPDATE

1. Open https://github.com/yogeshkrishna/ceiling-scout/releases/latest on your phone.
2. Download Ceiling-Scout.apk. Open it with Package Installer, then tap Install.
   Install over the old app. Do NOT uninstall: your build and calibration stay saved.
3. Open Ceiling Scout > Show floating bar > Open Corebound.
4. Select Lost Scrapyard boosted to Frozen five stars and press Run on the bar.
   Default: three Hookshots / seven jumps. Change equipment through Edit build & farming settings.
5. More controls are under the bar's ••• menu. Calibrate once if needed.
   Preview captured game lets you check that the bar is absent from the input image.
