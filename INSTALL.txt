CEILING SCOUT 0.6.0 — OFFLINE MAPS

UPDATE
Ceiling Scout > Check for updates > Download & install > confirm Install.
Or download Ceiling-Scout.apk from the release page and install over the old app.
Do not uninstall first: installing over it keeps your build and calibration.

RECORD AND BUILD — NO LAPTOP OR WI-FI NEEDED
1. Enable Ceiling Scout controls in Android Accessibility if disconnected.
2. Choose Use offline mapper > Record a map > allow Android screen capture.
3. Play the level yourself. Cover the floor, high ceilings, shafts and turns.
4. Tap Stop on the floating bar when your run is finished.
5. Open Ceiling Scout > Recorded maps · build, preview & export.
6. Tap Build / Resume map. Keep the app allowed to run in the background.
   Progress appears in the app and notification. Pause and resume when needed.
7. Once ready: View map & save PNG > select section > Save native PNG.
   Choose a destination, then wait for the export-complete message in Recorded maps.

The preview fits your display. Export keeps original pixel dimensions.
Uncertain joins stay separate. Unseen areas stay transparent.
Processing time depends on the recording and your phone; it can be substantial.
Original recordings stay on this phone. Do not clear app storage or uninstall.
Share original recording lets you send a ZIP of the evidence when needed.
After exporting, Delete this recording can free space; it asks before deleting.

OPTIONAL LAPTOP
Studio remains optional for live mapping. Use Connect laptop and Start live capture
under Optional · live laptop connection. Both devices need the same Wi-Fi for this mode.

FARMING
Use farmer > Lost Scrapyard with Frozen five stars > Run.
Build, Calibrate and Stop & hide are under the three-dot menu.
Default: three Hookshots / seven jumps. Edit your build in settings.
Recorded maps do not yet steer farming. Native scale is not guaranteed full coverage.
